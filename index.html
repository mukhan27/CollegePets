🏀 How to Hoop

Move with the joystick. Hold Shoot to raise the meter, release inside the green band for a GREEN! release — the best shot you can take.

Early / Late releases still drop sometimes; way off almost never. Jump first and the meter turns blue & ⚡ fast — harder to time, but a jump shot rises over defenders.

Where you shoot from matters. While you hold the ball, badges above the meter read the floor live:

LAYUP at the rim · MID inside the arc · 3PT beyond the painted 3-point line — worth 3

OPEN GUARDED SMOTHERED — defensive pressure. Smothered shots rarely fall; move or pass.

Dunk: jump right at the rim with the ball and tap Shoot when DUNK READY! flashes — no meter needed.

On defense: get close and face the ball-handler, then Steal. Block / Jump puts a hand up — a jumping defender wrecks badly-timed shots.

Passing: if a defender sits in the passing lane, the Pass button turns red — ⚠ lane covered. Passes thrown through a covered lane get intercepted.

Dribble-lock: stop moving after a dribble and you pick the ball up — from there you can only shoot, jump or pass.
